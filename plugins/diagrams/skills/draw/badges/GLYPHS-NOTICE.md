# Line glyphs

Most glyphs in `glyphs.json` (all but robot, shield, gear, person, doc, policy, target, chart, filter,
inbox and check, which are ours) are converted from Lucide (https://lucide.dev), npm `lucide-static`
1.68.0, by `badges/vendor.py`.

Lucide is under the ISC License, and the icons it takes from Feather are under the MIT License; both
notices are copied in full in `LUCIDE-LICENSE` next to this file.
